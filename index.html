The problem

Founder Bottleneck Syndrome

Growth stalls when every important decision still runs through the founder. The signs:

  1. Decisions queue for your approval, and the team waits.
  2. Delivery slows as the team grows, not faster.
  3. Technical debt from the early days now dictates the roadmap.
  4. Hiring is ad hoc, and new people take months to become effective.
  5. You're the only one who knows how things really work.

Three fixes

What I fix

Process

A delivery and product process the team owns, so work flows without you approving every step.

Technical debt

A clear-eyed assessment and a plan that pays down what is slowing you, without stopping delivery.

Hiring

The roles, the bar and the process to build a team that can lead itself.

Every engagement is designed to end.
